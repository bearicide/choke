Quick start

  1. Tap LOAD CRATE and pick a folder full of sounds.
  2. CHOKE runs Smart Fill and makes a playable 16-pad kit.
  3. Hit pads on screen, keyboard, or MIDI.
  4. Tap LOCK on anything good.
  5. Tap REROLL UNLOCKED to replace the weak stuff.
  6. Use PANIC when the machine gets ideas.

Keyboard pads: 1 2 3 4 / Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C V. Hold Shift while pressing a pad key to loop it.

FAQ

What is CHOKE?

A fast sample-pad page. It takes sounds, spreads them across pads, and lets one sound cut off another so things stay punchy instead of messy.

What does Smart Fill do?

It looks at names and folders, then guesses where sounds belong: drums first, loops next, vocals and FX after that, weird stuff at the bottom.

Why did my sound stop?

That is the choke. CHOKE keeps new hits clean by cutting older hits. Turn a pad into a loop only when you want it to keep running.

Why no sound?

Press any button first, then load or play. Browsers make audio sleep until you touch the page. Also check volume, Bluetooth, and the panic button.

Why is MIDI weird?

Different controllers send different pad numbers. Try the map dropdown. If a note is unknown, CHOKE will still catch it and show the note in the monitor.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes for touch pads and loaded audio. MIDI is best on desktop Chrome or Edge. Phone browsers are moodier than a raccoon with a mortgage.

Does it upload my samples?

No. The sounds stay in your browser while the page is open. Export only saves a map of what CHOKE assigned.
